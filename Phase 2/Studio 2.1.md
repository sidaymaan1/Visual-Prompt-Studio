Medium A Prompt - Create a watercolor painting of a futuristic city at sunset with towering glass skyscrapers, floating vehicles, glowing neon lights, and a reflective river running through the city. Emphasize color, line, contrast, texture, and movement by using soft, transparent washes and layered gradients. Use watercolor brushes and the wet-on-wet technique to create smooth color transitions in the sky, while using layering to add depth to the buildings and reflections. Warm orange and pink sunset tones should blend into cool blues and purples, creating a sense of harmony and unity. The composition should guide the viewer's eye from the glowing skyline to the river, creating a peaceful yet futuristic atmosphere.

Medium B Prompt - Create a linocut print of the same futuristic city at sunset with towering skyscrapers, floating vehicles, glowing lights, and a reflective river. Emphasize line, contrast, texture, movement, and unity using bold carved lines and simplified geometric shapes. Use linoleum carving tools and the relief printing process to create strong black-and-white contrast with dramatic shadows. Repeated carved textures should suggest reflections on the water and architectural details while thick, expressive lines lead the viewer's eye through the city. The composition should remain identical to the watercolor version but communicate a more graphic and dramatic visual style.
