Assignment title: Visual Prompt Studio Phase 1.1

Prompt: Create a digital illustration movie poster for a science fiction blockbuster. The setting is an abandoned deep-sea research laboratory that has been taken over by glowing alien plants and mysterious sea creatures. The composition should use long, dramatic directional lines leading toward the laboratory entrance while irregular organic lines formed by vines and tentacles create movement throughout the scene. Large geometric shapes make up the research buildings, while flowing organic shapes dominate the alien life. The laboratory and diver occupy the positive space, while the surrounding dark ocean creates large areas of negative space to make the poster feel isolated and mysterious. The surfaces should have sleek metallic walls covered with rough coral and textured alien growth. The laboratory should appear massive and heavy, giving it a strong sense of volume. Use intense contrast by placing a bright glowing blue light at the laboratory entrance against the dark ocean so that the entrance becomes the clear focal point of the poster. The overall mood should feel suspenseful, mysterious, and cinematic.
